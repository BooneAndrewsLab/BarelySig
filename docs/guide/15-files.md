# Saving, files and privacy

## Your work is kept as you go

Every change is kept in this browser, a second after you make it. Close
the tab, come back tomorrow, and the project is where you left it. The bar
says **Saved in this browser**.

**Projects** lists the projects kept in this browser; **New project**
starts another and **Close project** goes back to the start screen, where
they are listed too.

The browser's storage belongs to this browser on this computer: another
browser or another computer doesn't see it, and clearing the browser's
site data deletes it. So keep [a file](#files) of anything that matters.

## Files

- **Download** (`Ctrl+S`) saves the project as a `.bsig` file: the tables,
  the analyses and their results, the graphs and the export history, in
  one file. Send it to a colleague or keep it with your lab notebook.
  The bar says **Downloaded** until you change something.
- **Open…** (`Ctrl+O`), or dropping a file on the window, opens a `.bsig`
  file — or a figure exported from BarelySig with its data, see
  [Exporting figures](14-export.md). An opened file becomes a new project
  in this browser; the file itself is never changed.

A `.bsig` file is plain text (JSON), so it can be read without BarelySig.

## Privacy

- Your data never leave your computer. The statistics run in your
  browser; there is no server, no upload and no account.
- BarelySig counts, anonymously, which features are used ("ran a t test",
  "exported an SVG") to know what to improve — never a value, a name, a
  title or anything you typed. No cookies are used, and nothing is counted
  when your browser asks sites not to track you.
